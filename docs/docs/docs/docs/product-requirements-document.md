# House of Lady Amour

Version: 1.0

Author: Marsharine A. Simpson

Status: Planning

---

# Executive Summary

The House of Lady Amour is an immersive lifestyle platform that combines publishing, storytelling, recipes, video, e-commerce, and inspired living into one elegant digital experience.

Rather than presenting products as traditional online store categories, the website invites visitors to explore themed spaces within the House of Lady Amour, creating an engaging and memorable brand experience.

---

# Problem Statement

The Lady Amour brand currently spans multiple platforms, including books, YouTube videos, recipes, and future products. These experiences are disconnected, making it difficult for visitors to discover everything the brand offers.

A unified platform is needed to bring all content, products, and experiences together under one cohesive digital home.

---

# Vision Statement

Create an elegant digital estate where visitors can discover books, recipes, videos, products, and inspiration through a welcoming, room-based experience that reflects the Lady Amour brand.
